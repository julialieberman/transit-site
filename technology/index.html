The viewer and notes search calculate in your browser. A live AI chat service is not connected.

Research, with a source attached.

Product pages and manufacturer manuals help identify parts and constraints. AI helps compare the information and keep unanswered questions visible. A plausible answer is not a measurement.

Manufacturer evidence first. Physical confirmation still required.

The story, with its evidence.

The website connects each published story to its selected parts, recorded costs, and open questions. The private project notebook is not published here.

Only curated stories and approved images belong on the site.

Models you can explore.

The viewer uses geometry from the two saved IdeaBuilder concepts. Select a member to identify its profile, length, and product page. Try another garage height to see the dimensional trade-off, without changing the saved design.

The visualization does not calculate structural strength, approve attachments, or establish physical fit.

Explore the models

Questions with context.

Search the published build notes or prepare an AI brief that includes a design option, the relevant source links, and unresolved questions. You can review the brief before copying it to an assistant of your choice.

No question or project data is automatically sent to an external AI service. A visitor-facing AI chat would need a separately configured service, cost controls, and a clear data policy.
