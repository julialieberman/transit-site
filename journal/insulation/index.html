Insulation before the interior finish

A pre-cut 3M Thinsulate kit and sound-deadening material were installed throughout the cargo area.

Installed by Dan Moore in Salt Lake City.

The receipt record separates the insulation kit, sound-deadening material, and labor. That makes this one of the better documented early systems even though the exact product number and hidden coverage still need a physical check.

The visible cavities show insulation in place, but future finish work still needs to preserve access long enough to check gaps, compression, moisture condition, and every system that will disappear behind panels.

This is a record of the build, not an installation guide. Reported completion does not establish inspection, structural adequacy, or safety.
