Bed platform designTwo ways to support the east-west bedIn progress

Two to-scale 80/20 concepts now compare the compact FarOutRide-inspired approach with a Ford-guided floor-supported frame.

Both options use two 97 in north-south side rails, four provisional 69 in east-west crossmembers, a nominal 60 x 78 in deck envelope, and 36 in of central garage clearance beneath the crossmembers. The deck is intended to extend into the GoCode Innie pockets without loading or touching the ABS capsules.

Ford's body-builder guidance makes the floor-supported option the leading candidate: upper wall fixing locations are non-structural, and a racking-style frame should be self-supporting with a verified floor load path. Exact floor anchors, wall ties, HEST dimensions, headroom, Innie clearance, and underfloor obstructions still require physical measurement and qualified review before ordering.
