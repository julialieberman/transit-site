Bed platform designTwo ways to support the east-west bedIn progress

I am comparing two aluminum frames for an east-west bed: a wall-assisted concept inspired by FarOutRide and a floor-supported alternative informed by Ford's guidance.

Both options use two 97 in north-south side rails, four provisional 69 in east-west crossmembers, a nominal 60 x 78 in deck envelope, and 36 in of central garage clearance beneath the crossmembers. The deck is intended to extend into the GoCode Innie pockets without loading or touching the ABS capsules.

Ford's body-builder guidance makes the floor-supported option the leading candidate: upper wall fixing locations are non-structural, and a racking-style frame should be self-supporting with a verified floor load path. Exact floor anchors, wall ties, HEST dimensions, headroom, Innie clearance, and underfloor obstructions still require physical measurement and qualified review before ordering.

HeatHeat from under the passenger seatReported complete

An Espar AS3-B2L gasoline air heater is installed under the passenger seat, according to the build record.

The installation record describes the heater, controller location, under-floor power routing, and warm-air outlet behind the seat base. It is one of the largest single climate-control investments in the build.

Because it is fuel-fired, the story is not finished at installation. The exact kit, fuel pickup, intake, exhaust, floor sealing, and commissioning remain inspection items, and the van needs working carbon-monoxide protection before the heater is relied on.

Installed by Dan Moore in Salt Lake City.

Shore powerConnecting to shore powerReported complete

The van has a shore-power connection for external AC power. The exact inlet and protection equipment still need to be identified.

I have not yet documented the inlet, cord, breaker, transfer behavior, grounding, or which loads the connection supplies.

The connection is reported as installed, but the technical details remain open. Identifying the inlet and having the protection path reviewed comes before recommending the setup or linking to an exact product.

Installed by Dan Moore in Salt Lake City.
