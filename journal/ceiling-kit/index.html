Planning the ceiling supports

I have the AVC RIG ceiling-furring brackets and wall-to-ceiling supports. I am checking the parts and their installation order before attaching them permanently.

The parts are intended to turn the curved Transit shell into a flatter, serviceable surface for the finished ceiling and upper wall transition. The current work is identification and sequencing, not a finished installation.

The exact EXT kit contents, rear trim piece, fasteners, corrosion isolation, wiring clearance, and relationship to the separate cabinet structure remain open. Permanent attachment waits until those questions are resolved.

The separately recorded GoCode full interior trim kit is included here as related finish work. It is not part of the AVC ceiling-support kit or the GoCode Innie sleeper pair used in the bed discussion.

This is a record of the build, not an installation guide. Reported completion does not establish inspection, structural adequacy, or safety.
