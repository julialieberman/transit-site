In progressThe ceiling system is the next major interior layerIn progress

AVC RIG ceiling-furring brackets and wall-to-ceiling supports are owned and being reconciled before permanent installation.

The parts are intended to turn the curved Transit shell into a flatter, serviceable surface for the finished ceiling and upper wall transition. The current work is identification and sequencing, not a finished installation.

The exact EXT kit contents, rear trim piece, fasteners, corrosion isolation, wiring clearance, and relationship to the separate cabinet structure remain open. The parts can be dry-fitted and photographed, but permanent attachment waits for those questions.

In progressCabinet bracing started, then stopped for a better planIn progress

The Timber overhead cabinet structural kit is owned, and an installation attempt exposed unresolved fastening and overlap questions.

The steel structure is meant to create a straight attachment line for future overhead cabinets. Holes were started for the supplied rivets, but the manual rivet tool did not work and no completed brace installation is recorded.

That pause is useful. Before work resumes, the exact kit revision, every drilled location, the rivet material and grip range, hidden-utility clearance, bare-edge treatment, and interaction with the AVC ceiling parts all need to be resolved.
