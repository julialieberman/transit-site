What this model can tell you

The original frame shapes come from the two saved IdeaBuilder concepts. Changes are temporary experiments, not revisions to the saved designs. The van skeleton is a nominal cargo reference with illustrative ribs, not a measured body scan.

Representative hardware assemblies show brackets, bolts, T-nuts, anchor cams and candidate floor bases. They are enlarged educational geometry, not manufacturer CAD or precise hole locations. Exploded views explain the pieces, not an approved assembly order. Removing a member also removes its dependent connection groups; it does not make the remaining structure viable.

Changing the height or profiles is a local geometry experiment. It does not prove strength, attachment compatibility, equipment clearance, or seated headroom. No external AI service receives your changes.
